A USEFUL STARTING POINT

A little context goes a long way.

  1. The question

    What decision or business process should this help with?

  2. The data

    Where does it live, and what formats are you working with?

  3. The result

    What would a useful deliverable look like? Include any target date or budget range you want me to consider.

A description is enough to start. We can agree on access and how to handle sensitive information before sharing files.
