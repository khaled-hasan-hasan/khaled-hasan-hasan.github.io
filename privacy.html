A simple approach
to privacy.

On this website

The portfolio does not include analytics trackers, advertising cookies, account registration or a contact-submission backend. Fonts and project images are served with the site.

Your inquiry draft

The optional draft builder processes text in your browser. It does not save it to browser storage or send it to a website server. Choosing “Open email draft” passes it to your email application, where your email provider’s policies apply.

External services

GitHub, Kaggle and LinkedIn links take you to external services with their own privacy policies. The hosting provider may process ordinary connection and request logs to operate and secure the website.
